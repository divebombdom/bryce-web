Quick guide

Bryce Web is a homage to the 1994 landscape program. Build in wireframe, watch the Nano-Preview, then press Render to ray trace the full image in progressively finer passes.

  • Create — click an icon to drop a water, cloud or ground plane, a fractal terrain, a stone, or a primitive into the world.
  • Edit — open the Materials Lab or the Terrain Editor, or switch the gizmo between move, rotate and resize.
  • Sky & Fog — drag the sun on the trackball, scrub the thumbnails for haze, fog and clouds, or pick a sky from memory.
